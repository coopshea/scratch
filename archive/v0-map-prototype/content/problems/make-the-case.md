## The argument, in order
1. Persistent contrails are a measurable share of warming [[radiative-balance]].
2. The night ones are unambiguous [[night-vs-day]].
3. The fix is a small altitude change [[avoidance]].
4. The benefit is local, so the country that acts is the country that gains [[incentives]].

## What would kill it
A public fight over daytime contrails that might be cooling. Keep the claim narrow: night flights, confident regions.
