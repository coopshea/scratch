## What is known
Per Platt: contrail effects are local, unlike CO2, so a country fixing contrails over itself improves its own climate. That asymmetry is the political lever. The reverse asymmetry is weak: there are almost no places where contrails are confidently cooling, so nobody has a reason to want more of them.
