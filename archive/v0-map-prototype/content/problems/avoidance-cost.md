  economics: Marginal fuel per reroute times fuel price, against the value of avoided forcing at whatever carbon price you believe. The ratio decides the program.
  physics: Lower altitude means denser air, more drag, and a less efficient engine operating point. The CO2 from the extra fuel is a small warming that partly offsets the avoided contrail.
  narrative: The cost is a rounding error on a ticket. The trick is making someone want to pay it.

## What is known
Per Platt: the fuel cost is small but real. Aviation is one of the hard cases for decarbonization because batteries do not have the energy density for flight, so contrail avoidance is one of the few aviation climate levers available now.

## How you would know you made progress
A cost curve: cumulative forcing avoided versus cumulative extra fuel, ranked by [[ranking-metric]]. The knee of that curve is the size of the program.
