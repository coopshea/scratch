# Notice

Something happened that needs you: said where it happened, in one line, with the way out beside it.

`.notice` on `radius-panel`: a title in `text-label`, then actions. `.is-error` on `objection-soft` with an `objection` title; `.is-info` on `pencil-soft`. `.inline-error` is one red line with a quiet retry, for small failures.

**Out of credits** sits under the spill, not in a dialog. Its buttons buy credits and come straight back and cut the spill. How pricing works is explained on the account page, not here.

**Copy**: what happened, then what's safe. No apologies, codes or "Oops". Never `alert()` or `confirm()`.
