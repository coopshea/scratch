# EmptyHint

What goes in an empty space, said once, in the space itself, in blue pencil. It goes the moment the space has something in it.

On the spill page it is two lines: a big serif line where the first words would go, then one `text-hint` line. On the board it sits under the dividing-cells animation (see the Spill screen): groups of ideas splitting and merging, in `pencil-soft` with a `pencil-line` edge.

**The words**
- Spill page: "Braindump here." / "Type, speak or paste everything you're thinking. Fragments are fine."
- Spill board: "Ideas gather here in loose groups. Let's get those ducks in a row."
- Shape, before anything is placed, in the first level only: "Drag an idea here to start."
- A spill that yields nothing: "Nothing to cut up here."

**Rules**: one hint per space, short, no how-it-works explanations; no tours, pop-ups or coach marks; gone once the space has content. Labels, counts and "stays out"-style commentary are not hints; leave them out.
